# Amazon Rainforest Tour to Manu National Park from Cusco

The Manu National Park is one of the most biodiverse places on Earth. Located in the southeastern region of Peru, this UNESCO World Heritage Site spans over 1.5 million hectares of pristine Amazon rainforest, cloud forest, and highland puna.

## Wildlife of Manu National Park

When you take an Amazon rainforest tour to Manu National Park from Cusco, you enter one of the last truly wild places on our planet. The park is home to:

- **1,000+ species of birds** — more than the entire United States
- **200+ species of mammals** — including the elusive jaguar, giant river otter, and tapir
- **300+ species of fish** — filling the rivers and oxbow lakes
- **Thousands of insect species** — many yet to be catalogued by science

## What to Expect on Your Tour

Our guided tours depart from Cusco and follow the dramatic mountain road through the cloud forest — a different ecosystem entirely from the lowland rainforest, but equally spectacular. As you descend, the temperature rises and the vegetation becomes increasingly lush.

### Day 1: The Cloud Forest

The first stage of the journey takes you through misty cloud forest at elevations between 2,000m and 3,500m. Keep your eyes open for the brilliant Andean cock-of-the-rock — Peru's national bird — as well as hummingbirds, orchids, and bromeliads.

### The Lowland Amazon

Once you reach the lowland rainforest, the Amazon reveals itself in all its glory. Boat trips on the river allow you to spot caimans basking on sandbanks, capybaras grazing along the riverside, and massive river otters fishing in the currents.
