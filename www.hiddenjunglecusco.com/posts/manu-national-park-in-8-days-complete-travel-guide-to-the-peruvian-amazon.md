# Manu National Park in 8 Days: Complete Travel Guide

A trip to Manu National Park in Peru is one of the great wildlife experiences on our planet. If you have 8 days available, you can experience everything Manu has to offer — from the dramatic Andean cloud forest to the depths of the lowland Amazon rainforest.

## Why 8 Days?

Eight days in Manu allows you to:
- Experience the full cloud forest to lowland rainforest transition
- Visit the famous Blanquillo macaw clay lick at dawn
- Explore remote oxbow lakes by kayak
- Have multiple river safaris searching for giants otters and caimans
- Enjoy the slower pace that allows wildlife to find you

Shorter tours are wonderful, but 8 days gives the forest time to reveal its secrets.

## Day-by-Day Overview

### Day 1 — Cusco to the Cloud Forest
Your adventure begins with a 6:00 AM departure from Cusco. The road climbs through Andean highlands before descending into the cloud forest. Stop at the famous cock-of-the-rock lek to watch Peru's national bird perform its remarkable courtship display.

### Day 2 — Cloud Forest to Lowland Lodge
Continue the descent through increasingly lush vegetation. Arrive at your first lodge in the lower cloud forest zone. Evening walk along the river.

### Day 3 — First River Safari
Your first full day in the Amazon. Morning boat safari on the river: watch for giant river otters, caimans, capybaras, and an incredible variety of birds. Afternoon jungle walk on forest trails.

### Day 4 — Blanquillo Macaw Clay Lick
Pre-dawn departure by boat to witness one of the natural world's great spectacles: hundreds of colorful macaws descending to the clay lick at sunrise to consume mineral-rich clay. Unforgettable.

### Day 5 — Oxbow Lake Exploration
Kayak to a remote oxbow lake hidden in the forest. Giant otters often fish here in family groups. Watch hoatzins in the lakeside vegetation and look for anacondas in the shallows.

### Day 6 — Deep Forest Trekking
A longer forest hike wearing rubber boots. Our guide reads the forest like a book — every footprint, broken branch, and sound has a story. Look for primates, peccaries, and tapir tracks.

### Day 7 — Night Walk & Fishing
A specialist night walk to spot nocturnal wildlife: tree frogs, night herons, and sleeping birds. Finish the day with piranha fishing — yes, they are edible, and delicious!

### Day 8 — Dawn Birding & Return to Cusco
Final morning birding session before breakfast. Then the long but beautiful drive back to Cusco, arriving in the late afternoon or early evening.
