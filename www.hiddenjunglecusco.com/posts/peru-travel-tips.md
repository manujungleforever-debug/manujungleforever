# Peru Travel Tips

Peru is a country of stunning landscapes, rich history, and diverse cultures. From the snow-capped peaks of the Andes to the depths of the Amazon rainforest, from the colonial grandeur of Cusco to the ancient mysteries of Machu Picchu — Peru has something extraordinary for every traveler.

Here are our essential tips to make the most of your Peruvian adventure.

## Before You Go

### Visa
Most nationalities (including US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia) do not require a visa for Peru for stays under 90 days. Check with your country's foreign ministry for current requirements.

### Vaccinations
Consult a travel health clinic 6-8 weeks before departure. Recommended vaccinations typically include:
- Hepatitis A and B
- Typhoid
- Yellow fever (required for Amazon travel — get the certificate)
- Rabies (for extended Amazon stays)
- COVID-19 (check current requirements)

### Travel Insurance
Essential. Make sure your policy covers: medical evacuation, adventure activities (jungle trekking, boat safaris), and altitude-related illness.

## Money

**Currency:** Peruvian Sol (PEN)  
**Credit cards:** Widely accepted in Cusco, Lima, and major tourist areas. Carry cash for smaller towns and all jungle areas.  
**ATMs:** Available throughout Cusco. Withdraw enough cash before departing for the Amazon — there are no ATMs in the jungle.

## Getting Around

### Internal Flights
Peru has a good domestic flight network. Lima-Cusco is the most important route (approximately 1.5 hours vs 20+ hours by bus).

### Buses
Long-distance buses in Peru range from basic to luxury (cama class — fully reclining seats). Cruz del Sur and Oltursa are reliable operators for overnight routes.

### Taxis
Always agree on the price before getting in. In Cusco, expect to pay 5-10 soles for journeys within the city.

## The Amazon

If you're visiting Peru, the Amazon is a must. The Manu National Park region accessible from Cusco is far less visited than the popular Iquitos region (which is only accessible by plane or river), making for a more authentic experience.

**Key tips for Amazon travel:**
- Book with a reputable local operator (like us!)
- Bring insect repellent and apply it religiously
- Stay hydrated — the humidity is intense
- Keep medicines sealed in waterproof bags
- Listen to your guides — they know the forest

## Health in the Amazon

- **Malaria:** Risk exists in some areas; consult your doctor about prophylaxis
- **Dengue fever:** No prophylaxis available; prevention is key (insect repellent, long clothing)
- **Intestinal issues:** Eat at reputable establishments; drink only treated or bottled water
- **Sun:** The equatorial sun is intense; sunscreen and hat are essential on boat trips

## Cusco Tips

- **Altitude:** Give yourself 2+ days to acclimatize before any strenuous activity
- **Sacred Valley:** Spend a night here before Cusco if possible — it's at lower altitude (2,800m) and gives you a gentler introduction
- **Best neighborhoods:** San Blas and the historic center are walkable and full of great restaurants and markets

## Food You Must Try

- **Ceviche** — Peru's national dish; fresh fish marinated in lime juice with chili
- **Lomo saltado** — stir-fried beef with tomatoes, onions, and French fries over rice
- **Chupe de camarones** — rich river prawn chowder (Arequipa specialty)
- **Cuy** — roasted guinea pig; a traditional Andean delicacy
- **Chicha morada** — a refreshing purple corn drink
