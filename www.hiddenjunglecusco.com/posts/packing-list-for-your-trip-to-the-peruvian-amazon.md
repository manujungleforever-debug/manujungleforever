# Packing List for Your Trip to the Peruvian Jungle

If you have already decided that for your next vacation you will take a trip to the Peruvian Amazon, congratulations — you are in for an extraordinary adventure. But packing for the jungle requires careful thought. Here's the complete guide to what you need.

## What to Wear

### Clothing
- **Lightweight, long-sleeved shirts** (2-3) — light colors preferred; protects from insects and sun
- **Long, lightweight trousers** (2 pairs) — convertible pants that zip off at the knee are ideal
- **Quick-dry underwear** — merino wool or synthetic; cotton stays wet for days
- **Warm layer** — the cloud forest is cold, even in the "summer"; bring a fleece or light down jacket
- **Rain poncho or lightweight rain jacket** — essential for afternoon showers
- **Wide-brimmed sun hat** — critical for river boat trips
- **Buff/neck gaiter** — protects from sun and insects on boat trips

### Footwear
- **Hiking boots** — waterproof and broken in; used for cloud forest sections
- **Sandals or flip flops** — for the lodge; your feet need to breathe
- **Rubber boots are provided** — we supply rubber boots for all jungle walks; you don't need to bring your own

## Essential Items

### Health & Hygiene
- **Insect repellent with DEET** (30-50%) — apply liberally; the Amazon is beautiful but insects are numerous
- **Sunscreen SPF 50+** — river boat trips involve intense sun exposure
- **Personal medications** — bring more than you need with clearly labeled packaging
- **Anti-malarial medication** — consult your doctor 4-6 weeks before departure
- **Water purification tablets** — as backup; we provide safe drinking water throughout
- **Basic first aid kit** — band-aids, antiseptic cream, blister treatment
- **Hand sanitizer and biodegradable soap**

### Electronics
- **Camera and extra batteries** — wildlife photography is demanding on batteries; bring at least 3 spares
- **Binoculars** — essential for birdwatching; 8x42 or 10x42 are ideal
- **Headlamp with extra batteries** — essential for night walks
- **Waterproof bag or dry sack** — for boat trips; electronics and cameras need protection
- **Power bank** — lodges have limited electricity, especially in remote areas

## What NOT to Bring

- Heavy books or laptops — leave them in Cusco
- Excessive jewelry or valuables
- White or bright colored clothing — tends to attract insects
- Spray-on insect repellent (aerosols) — bad for forest air quality; use roll-on or lotion

## Money & Documents

- Cash in Peruvian soles — no ATMs in the jungle; withdraw in Cusco before departure
- Your tour confirmation documents
- Travel insurance documentation
- Emergency contact information

## Pro Tips from Our Guides

1. **Layer up** — you'll experience three different climate zones (Andean, cloud forest, Amazon)
2. **Pack light** — you'll be carrying your bag; every kilo counts
3. **Seal everything in ziploc bags** — humidity and river spray will find their way into your bag
4. **Break in your boots** — new boots = blisters; wear them for several weeks before the trip
