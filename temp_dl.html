Jungle Wildlife Quest: Machu Wasi

3 Days / 2 Nights

Overview

This tour focuses on searching for animals in their natural habitat, getting a taste of the beautiful rainforest, and learning about the jungle’s intricate ecosystems.┬á

Highlights

Look for animals in the cloud forest, travel by boat on the river, see a parrot clay lick, birdwatching, night walk, nature hikes┬á

Day 2: Welcome to the Jungle

┬áIn the afternoon, we will visit nearby Machu Wasi to catch some wildlife in action. Machu Wasi is an Oxbow lake, meaning it used to be a part of the big river, but is now a serene and secluded area where animals can relax. At the lake, there are balsa rafts to ride around the river, and paths for hiking.┬á After dinner in the lodge, weÔÇÖll search for creepy crawlies like tarantulas and frogs on a night walk.

Here we hope to see flocks of green parrots having their morning clay feast for breakfast. When theyÔÇÖve had their fill weÔÇÖll head back to land and begin the journey back to Cusco.
