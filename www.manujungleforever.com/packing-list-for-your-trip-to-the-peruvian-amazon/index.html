PACKING LIST FOR YOUR TRIP TO THE PERUVIAN JUNGLE

PACKING LIST FOR YOUR TRIP TO THE PERUVIAN JUNGLE

Published by: Anna Ashley | December 31, 2022

If you have already decided that for your next vacation you will take a trip to the Peruvian jungle, surely you want to know the necessary items. Because remember, a forewarned and informed traveler is worth two. And we want you to have the best days of your life. So we leave you these tips on items that you should not leave out of the suitcase. 

Sunscreen

On your trip to the jungle of Peru, you will face several somewhat extreme climates that will add excitement to this adventure. And although many think that in the jungle it does not stop raining and that clouds fill the sky, the truth is that solar radiation penetrates our clothes even on days without sun. That’s why you must apply sunscreen at least three times a day. Do not forget, remember that the health of your skin is very important. 

Boots

A good pair of boots are essential for your trip to the Peruvian Amazon. Try to buy them in advance and wear them to ensure that they fit you well and that you can walk in them for several kilometers. We recommend that they be half a size larger than what you normally use and that you look for those with waterproof material so that your feet do not get wet. Currently, there are several options on the market, so do not wait any longer and give yourself the task of finding yours. 

Repellent

Mosquito repellent will make your vacation in the Peruvian jungle much more comfortable. As you well imagine, in the Peruvian jungle there are several insects that you don’t even know about, most of them harmless, although they are eager to fly and perch on you. That is why you must use a good lotion that keeps those strange beings away from you. We recommend that you get this product from a specialty safari or camping store. 

First aid kit

Taking a first-aid kit wherever you go on vacation is a practice that you should always keep in mind. Even more so, if the place you are traveling to includes places where there will not always be a doctor available at all times. And although the tours to the Amazon jungle are usually very safe, it never hurts to carry pills for pain, dizziness, fever, and some antiseptics. Remember that the safer you feel, the more you will enjoy these unforgettable days. 

 

Comfortable clothes

The clothes that you will take on your trip to the Peruvian Amazon can make the difference between a great vacation and others that are not so great. We recommend an outfit that covers most of your body although in a fresh way. Cotton clothing, hats, and long-sleeved shirts are essential. 

Now that you know these tips, we can feel your excitement for the upcoming trip. Don’t forget to take lots of photos and enjoy the moments
