WILDLIFE QUEST

7-Day Deep Amazon Wildlife Quest

7 Days / 6 Nights 2–8 Travelers Fácil
Overview

Experience the Wild Heart of Manu

An in-depth 7-day wildlife expedition deep into the pristine primary rainforest of Manu National Park, searching for jaguars, tapirs, giant river otters, and macaws.

Our 7-Day Deep Amazon Wildlife Quest takes you deep into the heart of the Manu Biosphere Reserve. Journey through the mystical cloud forest of the Andes down to the Amazonian lowlands. Navigate winding rivers, explore hidden oxbow lakes (cochas) on silent catamarans, visit world-renowned macaw clay licks (colpas), and spend nights listening to the symphony of the jungle at our comfortable rainforest lodges with professional bilingual naturalist guides.

Day-by-Day Journey

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Cusco → Andes Mountain Pass → Cloud Forest Lodge

Early morning pick-up from your hotel in Cusco (5:30 AM). Scenic drive over the Andes with stops at pre-Inca Ninamarca chullpas and the folkloric town of Paucartambo. Descend into the lush Cloud Forest of Manu to spot the Andean Cock-of-the-Rock (Peru's national bird), woolly monkeys, and orchids. Night walk near the lodge.

Day 2: Cloud Forest → Atalaya Port → Rainforest Lodge

Morning birdwatching walk. Continue driving to Atalaya river port, transition to motorized dugout canoe, and navigate down the Madre de Dios River. Spotting herons, kingfishers, and sunbathing caimans. Afternoon trek along primary forest trails with introduction to medicinal plants.

Day 3: Machu Wasi Oxbow Lake & Mammal Clay Lick

Dawn excursion to Machu Wasi Lake on traditional balsa rafts to observe prehistoric hoatzin birds, horned screamers, capybaras, and monkeys. In the evening, visit a natural tapir clay lick platform for a night vigil.

Day 4: Deep River Navigation → Blanquillo Biological Station

Embark on an extended boat expedition further downriver into deeper rainforest sectors. High probability of encountering river wildlife such as side-necked turtles, capybara families, and elusive jaguars on the beaches. Settle into Blanquillo Lodge.

Day 5: Blanquillo Macaw Clay Lick & Cocha Blanco Lake

Early 5:00 AM departure to the hidden macaw clay lick blind. Witness hundreds of red-and-green macaws, parrots, and parakeets gathering in a spectacle of color. Afternoon exploration of Cocha Blanco to search for the resident family of Giant River Otters (*Pteronura brasiliensis*).

Day 6: Canopy Tower Observation & Primary Rainforest Treks

Climb the 42-meter canopy observation tower for breathtaking panoramic views across the Amazon treetops, perfect for toucans, cotingas, and raptors. Afternoon deep forest exploration focusing on ancient giant ceiba trees and diverse monkey troops.

Day 7: Upriver Return Boat Trip → Cusco

Early morning upriver boat ride back to Atalaya port. Transfer to our private van for the scenic return journey through the Andes, arriving in Cusco around 7:00 PM.
